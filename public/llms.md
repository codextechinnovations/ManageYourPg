## Overview

MY PG is India's #1 PG (Paying Guest) management software, serving as a comprehensive platform for PG owners, landlords, and property managers to automate and streamline their operations. With a 4.8/5 star rating from over 5000 users, the platform manages 10,000+ PG properties and 50,000+ tenants across India.

## What is PG Management?

PG (Paying Guest) accommodation is a popular housing option in India, particularly for students and working professionals. PG owners typically manage multiple rooms with multiple beds, handle rent collection from dozens of tenants, track expenses, and maintain records. MY PG digitizes and automates these operations through a mobile app and web dashboard.

## Core Product Features

### 1. Tenant Management
- **Digital KYC Storage**: Secure storage of Aadhaar cards, photos, and address proofs
- **Tenant Profiles**: Complete profiles with contact details, payment history, and preferences
- **Room/Bed Assignment**: Track exactly which bed in which room each tenant occupies
- **Move-in/Move-out Management**: Streamlined processes for tenant lifecycle
- **Security Deposits**: Track deposits and automate refund calculations
- **Police Verification**: Integrated background screening support
- **Dietary Preferences**: Track food preferences for mess management

### 2. Rent Collection Automation
- **Automatic Reminders**: WhatsApp and app notifications sent on due dates
- **Payment Tracking**: Real-time dashboard showing paid/pending/overdue status
- **Digital Receipts**: Auto-generated rent receipts upon payment
- **Payment Integration**: UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm support
- **Reports**: Detailed collection reports with aging analysis
- **Reduces Late Payments**: By up to 80% through automation

### 3. Property & Room Management
- **Multi-Property Support**: Manage unlimited PG buildings from one account
- **Room Configuration**: Define single/double/triple sharing rooms
- **Bed Management**: Track individual bed occupancy
- **Occupancy Dashboard**: Visual representation of vacant vs occupied beds
- **Floor-wise Management**: Organize rooms by floor
- **Quick Property Switching**: Easy navigation between properties

### 4. Expense Management
- **Categorization**: Maintenance, electricity, water, food, staff salaries, etc.
- **Receipt Upload**: Attach bills and invoices to expenses
- **Monthly Reports**: Track spending patterns
- **Budget Alerts**: Get notified when expenses exceed thresholds
- **Profit/Loss Reports**: Understand property profitability

### 5. Additional Services
- **CCTV Integration**: Connect surveillance cameras to the dashboard
- **Food/Mess Management**: Meal plans, attendance, and billing
- **Cleaning Services**: Schedule and track housekeeping
- **Maintenance Requests**: Tenants can raise complaints through the app
- **Notice Board**: Send announcements to all tenants
- **Short Stay Support**: Manage daily/weekly/monthly rental options
